# Checkpoint B: decisions for the reviewer

These are the judgment calls the research surfaced. Each one is also a flagged row in the CSV.

## Red lines (strict rule: own words, official channel or recorded interview, explicitly a coalition condition)
1. **ישראל ביתנו · גיוס.** Liberman on X, 17.8.2026: "ישנם נושאים שלא נוותר עליהם, בין אם אני אכהן כראש ממשלה או כשר … חוק גיוס לכולם … יהיה החוק הראשון שהממשלה הבאה תחוקק." The wording is non-negotiable, but it is not literally "we won't join a government that…". The quote was taken from ynet and not checked against X.
2. **עמך ישראל · גיוס.** "חקיקת חוק זה לפני השבעת הממשלה מהווה תנאי סף עבור כניסתנו לכל ממשלה". This is an explicit coalition condition, but it comes from the party's written answer to the ynet questionnaire, not from its own channel.

2a. **ישראל ביתנו · חקירה.** Liberman in a Galei Tzahal interview (17.8.2026), as quoted by N12: "לא תהיה פה שום ויתור: לא בנושא גיוס לכולם, לא בוועדת חקירה ממלכתית…". A recorded interview qualifies; the wording is "no concession", the same as item 1.
2b. **ישר! (Eisenkot) red line not verified.** Israel Hayom reported he would not take partners who reject his principles, but the page was blocked. Left false.

3a. **עוצמה יהודית · מדיני/עזה (red line left false).** Its "התנתקות 710" plan (4.9.2026) quotes Ben Gvir saying a voluntary-emigration ministry "תהיה בראש הדרישות הקואליציוניות שלנו". That is a top demand, not "תנאי סף". Decide whether it counts as a red line.

## Placements to confirm
3. **הליכוד · גיוס (0, low).** Based on Netanyahu's interviews of 18–20.10.2026: a target of 23,000 in 3.5 years, and "מי שלא לומד שילך להתגייס". In the same period he also said "גיוס ללא כפיה". Open question: a July 2026 government law (חוק שירות ביטחון, תיקון 28) froze enforcement against yeshiva students until 30.11.2026. If a Likud minister led it, it is Likud `record` and would point to −1 or −2. Who led it was not confirmed.
4. **הציונות הדתית וזהות · גיוס (−1, low).** The components disagree: הציונות הדתית offers adapted tracks and incentives, while זהות rejects "גיוס כפוי". The list is placed on what they share. Null would also be defensible.
5. **הציונות הדתית וזהות · כלכלה (−1, low).** זהות's platform calls for broad tax cuts (−2), while the הציונות הדתית N12 answer focuses on competition (−1). The list is placed on what they share.
6. **פשיעה −2 cluster (7 parties).** הליכוד, ביחד, הדמוקרטים, עוצמה יהודית, ישראל ביתנו, הציונות הדתית וזהות and המילואימניקים והכלכלית are all at −2, mainly because each supports Shin Bet involvement. הדמוקרטים pairs that with a community track, so −1 would also be defensible. Decide whether supporting the Shin Bet alone is enough for −2.
7. **רע״ם · פשיעה (0, low).** Based on a Knesset speech (16.07.2026): fighting crime is outside the Shin Bet's legal authority. His position on the Shin Bet has shifted over the years. Null would also be defensible.
8. **הרשימה המשותפת · פשיעה (+1).** Could be +2: it opposes administrative detention but does not call for outside oversight of the police.

9. **הליכוד · מדיני (−1, med).** The Likud site says "חיזוק ההתיישבות והריבונות בכל חלקי הארץ", which could support −2. Placed at −1 because Netanyahu has not committed to applying sovereignty. The emigration line ("allowing Gazans who want to leave to leave", Fox News, Feb 2025) has not been checked against the primary source.
10. **רע״ם · מדיני (+2, med).** Mansour Abbas called for recognising an independent Palestinian state and ending the occupation (Tayibe, 22.8.2026). No 2026 mention of the 1967 lines, so +1 would also be defensible.
11. **עזה, −1 vs 0 close calls:** ישראל ביתנו at −1 (destroy Hamas, then an external body); ביחד at 0 (hold the yellow line until disarmament, bring in Egypt); ישר! at 0 (Eisenkot 2024, plus his Oct 2026 pledge to end Hamas rule within 18 months).
12. **המילואימניקים והכלכלית and עמך ישראל · עזה (−1)** could be −2: the first would have Israeli communities farm up to the yellow line, the second takes territory from Gaza.

13. **חקירה boundaries:** עמך ישראל at −1 (judges elected by the Knesset in a secret vote, but full powers and binding conclusions, so 0 is arguable). ישר! and ביחד at +1, not +2: a state commission covering the political echelon, but no explicit commitment to implement its conclusions.
14. **הליכוד · חקירה (−1).** Record: the Kallner bill (BillID 2237628). Members are appointed by the Knesset and the government sets the topics; it passed first reading 59–0 on 6.7.2026. Likud, Otzma, Shas and UTJ declined to answer ynet's inquiry survey (4.10.2026).
15. **משפט judgment calls:** ביחד at 0 (regulate judicial review by broad agreement; its constitution pledge could make it +1). ש״ס at −2 (its own bill's 4-year sunset could argue −1). יהדות התורה at −2 rests on one 2023 bill. רע״ם at 0 rests on 2023 sources only. המילואימניקים והכלכלית at 0: the components differ on the override majority.
16. **Nulls:** הרשימה המשותפת · משפט (nothing on judge selection, override or the attorney general) and יהדות התורה · חקירה (only individual MKs, in 2024).

## Data notes
- Some undated official pages (המילואימניקים והכלכלית, עמך ישראל) have `sourceDate` left empty rather than an invented date.
- The coalition bloc has more nulls than the opposition, because the Haredi parties and עוצמה יהודית have no 2026 platform and gave few on-record statements. The methodology's coverage table discloses this.
- Gaza context used for placement (from the research agent): ceasefire since 10.10.2025 under the 20-point plan and UNSC 2803; all hostages returned by Jan 2026; the IDF holds about 60% of the Strip behind the "yellow line"; Hamas still controls the west; no real reconstruction yet. The latest confirmation found is from mid-September 2026.
- ש״ס and יהדות התורה are null on מדיני and עזה. They have no platform and no quoted statements; ynet only paraphrases them, which does not count as a source.
